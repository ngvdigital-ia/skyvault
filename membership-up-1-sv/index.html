Module 2

How to Use These AI?

How to Find Cheap Flights With Miles

Using AI to find cheap flights is simple and straightforward. Follow these steps to get the best results:

  1. Tell the AI where you want to fly from and to.
    Example: "São Paulo to Dubai."
  2. Say if it's one-way or round-trip, and tell the month you want to travel.
    This helps the AI narrow down the best options for your specific travel dates.
  3. Tell the AI the travel class (economy, premium economy, or business) and how many days you want to stay.
    Specifying your preferences helps the AI find the most suitable flights for your needs.

About Stops and Connections

The AI will always search for flights with 0 or 1 stop because they are faster, easier, and usually cheaper.

If you see flights with 2 or 3 stops, it means the airline or date is not ideal, and you should search again with different dates or airlines.

Example so simple a 5-year-old understands:

If you go from home to grandma's house and only stop once to drink water, it's easy. But if you need to stop three times, your trip takes much longer and becomes annoying. That's why the AI finds flights with zero or one stop, because it's better for you.

What the AI Will Give You

After you send your trip request, the AI gives you:

  • The cheapest option in miles
  • How many miles it costs
  • Which airline is offering it
  • A direct Seats.aero link to reserve

Important:

You don't need to have miles right now. You can convert your money into miles using apps or directly with the airline. This process is explained step-by-step in Module 2 of SkyVault.
