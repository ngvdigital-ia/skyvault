Where Seats.aero fits

Traditional flight search sites often prioritize cash fares and don't always make it easy to compare award availability across loyalty programs.

Seats.aero is presented here as a support tool to:

  • View award availability in certain programs
  • Compare dates and route variations using filters
  • Set alerts to track a route over time
